How we work

Buyers can’t afford surprises.

So we built the unglamorous parts properly: sealed product, clean documentation, labels that match the routing guide, and ship dates that hold.

Sourced

Bought from partners in Tokyo, Kobe and Seoul. Factory-sealed, never opened.

Imported

We handle export, air freight, customs and duties into the US.

Prepared

UPCs, carton labels and packing slips, built to each chain’s routing guide.

Shipped

Parcel or LTL from Miami Gardens, 5–7 business days from cleared payment.

What arrives at your DC

One SKU per carton when it can be. Every carton labeled and listed.

Each carton gets its own label and packing slip with PO, SKU, UPC and quantity, so receiving matches the ASN without opening a box.
